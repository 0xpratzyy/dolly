# Click sounds

Real recordings, all CC0 (public domain): free to use, change and ship, no credit required. Each was trimmed,
converted to mono 48 kHz and peak-normalised; nothing else was changed.

| File | From | Author | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `press.wav` | the press (104-200 ms) of `switch34.wav`, [UI SFX Set](https://opengameart.org/content/51-ui-sound-effects-buttons-switches-and-clicks) | Kenney ([kenney.nl](https://www.kenney.nl)) | CC0 |
| `release.wav` | the release (236-320 ms) of the same `switch34.wav`, at its natural level | Kenney | CC0 |
| `key.wav` | the first 45 ms (the press) of `click30.wav`, [87 Clickety Clips](https://opengameart.org/content/87-clickety-clips) | OpenGameArt uploader | CC0 |

Checked with earshot (realness): real recordings, p_real 0.96 (switch34) and 0.92 (click30); switch34 ranked first of 140 clips for "a computer mouse click".
